Why We Admire Emma

Emma Watson is admired for her talent, intelligence, and dedication to social causes. She has made a significant impact in the entertainment industry and beyond, using her platform to advocate for gender equality and women's rights. Her work as a UN Women Goodwill Ambassador and her involvement in the HeForShe campaign have inspired many to take action towards creating a more equitable world.

  • Played Hermione Granger in the Harry Potter film series
  • UN Women Goodwill Ambassador
  • Advocate for gender equality and women's rights
  • Graduated from Brown University with a degree in English Literature
