About Emma Watson

Biography

Emma Charlotte Duerden Watson is an English actress, model, and activist. She is known for her role as Hermione Granger in the Harry Potter film series and for her work as a UN Women Goodwill Ambassador.

Quick Facts

  • Born: April 15, 1990 (Paris, France)
  • Genres: Actress, Model, Activist
  • Breakthrough Role: Hermione Granger (2001-2011)
  • Debut Role: Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone (2001)

Awards & Nominations

  • 2017: MTV Movie & TV Awards (Best Actor in a Movie) - Won
  • 2014: British Academy Film Awards (British Artist of the Year) - Won

Iconic Quote

"It is not the ability to do great things that makes a difference, it is the choice to do them."
