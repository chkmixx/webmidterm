About Emma Watson

Biography

Emma Charlotte Duerden Watson is an English actress, model, and activist. She is known for her role as Hermione Granger in the Harry Potter film series and for her work as a UN Women Goodwill Ambassador.

Quick Facts

  • Born: April 15, 1990 (Paris, France)
  • Genres: Actress, Model, Activist
  • Breakthrough Role: Hermione Granger (2001-2011)
  • Debut Acting Role: Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone (2001)

Style & Aesthetic

Emma Watson's style is characterized by her elegant, classic look and her commitment to sustainable fashion. She often incorporates vintage elements into her outfits and is known for her advocacy for environmental causes.
